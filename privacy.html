Heroxalis — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 20, 2026 Effective Date: November 20, 2026 Organization: Heroxalis Organizational Nature: Independent Development Group Jurisdiction: Republic of the Philippines

Introduction

Heroxalis ("Heroxalis", "we", "our", or "us") is an independent software development and technology-oriented group engaged in the research, development, maintenance, and operation of software applications, artificial intelligence systems, websites, digital platforms, and other technology-driven projects.

This Privacy Policy establishes the general principles and practices governing the collection, processing, storage, use, disclosure, retention, and protection of personal information in connection with Heroxalis and its associated projects and services.

Heroxalis recognizes the importance of protecting personal information and respecting the privacy rights of individuals who interact with its websites, applications, services, platforms, and other development projects.

This policy is established with reference to the applicable laws and regulations of the Republic of the Philippines, particularly Republic Act No. 10173, otherwise known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012, its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and applicable issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

This Privacy Policy applies to Heroxalis-operated services unless a particular project publishes a separate or supplementary Privacy Policy.

Individual projects may have additional privacy requirements depending on their functionality, technical implementation, infrastructure, and operational arrangements.

1. Scope and Applicability

This Privacy Policy applies to individuals who interact with Heroxalis through its official websites, applications, platforms, software, communication channels, and other services operated or maintained under the Heroxalis identity.

Covered activities may include:

  • Accessing and using official Heroxalis websites.
  • Interacting with Heroxalis applications and software.
  • Using artificial intelligence systems and conversational services.
  • Interacting with bots and platform integrations.
  • Participating in project communities and support channels.
  • Submitting inquiries, reports, feedback, or technical requests.
  • Participating in development activities or project contributions.

This policy does not automatically govern independent third-party platforms, websites, or services that Heroxalis does not own or control.

Where a project maintains its own Privacy Policy, that document shall supplement this general policy and describe the project's specific information-processing practices.

2. Information We May Collect

The categories of information processed by Heroxalis depend on the specific service, functionality, and interaction involved.

We seek to collect and process only information that is reasonably necessary for legitimate and declared purposes.

Depending on the project, information may include the following:

2.1. Account and Identification Information

Information voluntarily provided or generated when accessing services may include:

  • Usernames and display names.
  • Account identifiers.
  • Email addresses, where provided.
  • Platform-specific identifiers.
  • Account registration information.
  • Authentication-related information.
  • Other information necessary for account administration.

2.2. User-Submitted Information and Communications

When users interact with Heroxalis services, we may process information they voluntarily submit, including:

  • Messages and communications.
  • Commands and prompts.
  • Questions and inquiries.
  • Feedback and suggestions.
  • Uploaded content and attachments, where supported.
  • Reports and support requests.
  • Other information submitted through available interfaces.

2.3. Technical and Diagnostic Information

Depending on the service, technical information may include:

  • System and application logs.
  • Error reports and diagnostic records.
  • Service interaction records.
  • Request timestamps.
  • Device or browser information, where collected.
  • Network and connection information, where necessary.
  • Security-related records.
  • Information required to identify technical failures.

2.4. Platform Integration Information

Heroxalis projects may integrate with third-party platforms such as Discord or other communication and application services.

Where applicable, these integrations may process:

  • Platform user identifiers.
  • Usernames and display names.
  • Server, workspace, or community identifiers.
  • Channel or communication identifiers.
  • Commands and interactions.
  • Permissions and configuration information.
  • Other platform data required for the requested functionality.

The availability of such information depends on the permissions granted and the technical capabilities of the relevant platform.

2.5. Artificial Intelligence Interaction Information

Artificial intelligence projects may process information submitted through conversational interfaces.

Depending on the implementation, this may include:

  • Text prompts and messages.
  • Conversation history, where enabled.
  • User-provided context.
  • Interaction preferences, where supported.
  • Audio input and generated audio, where voice features are available.
  • Other information necessary to generate or maintain AI interactions.

AI-related information processing shall be governed by the relevant project's privacy disclosures.

2.6. Website and Usage Information

Where website analytics, cookies, or similar technologies are implemented, Heroxalis may process information concerning website interactions, such as:

  • Pages visited.
  • General interaction patterns.
  • Browser and device characteristics.
  • Session information.
  • Technical performance information.
  • Cookie or similar identifier information.

The specific technologies used and their purposes shall be disclosed where applicable.

Heroxalis does not represent that every category listed in this section is collected by every project.

3. Purposes of Processing

Personal information may be processed for legitimate and specified purposes, including:

  • Providing requested software functionality and services.
  • Operating and maintaining applications and platforms.
  • Responding to user requests and communications.
  • Processing commands, prompts, and interactions.
  • Maintaining account and access functionality.
  • Providing technical assistance and troubleshooting.
  • Monitoring system performance and reliability.
  • Identifying and preventing abuse, fraud, or unauthorized access.
  • Maintaining service security and operational integrity.
  • Communicating important service-related information.
  • Evaluating and improving existing functionality.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.
  • Establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims where necessary.

Information shall not be processed for purposes incompatible with the original declared purposes without an appropriate lawful basis.

4. Lawful Basis for Processing

Heroxalis recognizes that personal information must be processed in accordance with an applicable lawful basis under Section 12 of Republic Act No. 10173.

Depending on the circumstances, processing may rely on:

4.1. Consent

Where consent is the applicable lawful basis, the individual must be appropriately informed and provide consent in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

4.2. Contractual Necessity

Processing may be necessary to perform an agreement with an individual or to take steps requested by that individual before entering into an agreement.

4.3. Legal Obligation

Processing may be necessary to comply with an obligation imposed by applicable law.

4.4. Vital Interests

Processing may be necessary to protect the life or health of an individual.

4.5. Public Authority or Emergency Requirements

Processing may be necessary to respond to a national emergency or fulfill applicable public authority functions, where legally relevant.

4.6. Legitimate Interests

Processing may be necessary to pursue legitimate interests, provided that such interests are not overridden by the fundamental rights and freedoms of the data subject.

The applicable lawful basis shall be determined according to the specific processing activity.

The processing of sensitive personal information and privileged information shall be subject to the additional restrictions and lawful conditions established under Section 13 of the Act.

5. Data Processing and Artificial Intelligence

Certain Heroxalis projects involve artificial intelligence, machine learning, and automated computational systems.

When users interact with these systems, submitted information may be processed to generate responses, maintain conversational context, execute requested functionality, or provide other supported services.

Depending on the implementation, processing may occur through:

  • Independently maintained AI models.
  • Privately operated inference infrastructure.
  • External AI service providers.
  • Application programming interfaces.
  • Supporting backend systems.
  • Third-party hosting or computational services.

The specific processing arrangements shall depend on the relevant project.

The availability of an AI system does not automatically mean that user-submitted information is used to train or fine-tune its underlying model.

Any use of personal information for model training, fine-tuning, evaluation, or other secondary purposes shall be subject to applicable legal requirements, appropriate disclosures, and an applicable lawful basis.

Project-specific AI privacy practices shall be disclosed in the corresponding Privacy Policy.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Heroxalis may store or temporarily process information through application memory, databases, server infrastructure, technical logs, and other storage systems.

The actual storage arrangements depend on the requirements and implementation of each project.

Personal information shall be retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the declared purposes, establish, exercise, or defend legal claims, comply with legal obligations, or satisfy other lawful retention requirements.

Retention considerations may include:

  • Operational requirements.
  • Account administration.
  • Technical troubleshooting.
  • Security monitoring.
  • Service maintenance.
  • Legal obligations.
  • Resolution of disputes.
  • Other legitimate and declared purposes.

Information that is no longer necessary shall be securely disposed of, destroyed, anonymized, or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable legal requirements and appropriate technical procedures.

Heroxalis does not promise automatic deletion, immediate erasure, or zero data retention unless the relevant functionality has been implemented and verified.

Individual projects may establish additional retention periods in their respective privacy documentation.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Heroxalis does not intend to sell personal information.

Personal information may be disclosed or made accessible to third parties only where necessary and supported by an applicable lawful basis or legal requirement.

Potential recipients may include:

7.1. Infrastructure and Hosting Providers

External providers may process information necessary to operate websites, applications, databases, servers, and other infrastructure.

7.2. AI and Technology Providers

Certain projects may rely on external AI models, APIs, machine learning services, and other technologies.

Information necessary to provide requested functionality may be transmitted to these providers where applicable.

7.3. Communication Platforms

Platforms used to access Heroxalis services may independently process information according to their own policies and terms.

7.4. Legal and Regulatory Authorities

Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law, lawful orders, regulatory obligations, or other legally recognized circumstances.

7.5. Security and Protection

Information may be processed or disclosed where reasonably necessary to investigate security incidents, prevent abuse, protect users, or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Heroxalis shall seek to apply appropriate safeguards when engaging third-party providers that process personal information on its behalf.

8. International Data Transfers

Certain Heroxalis projects may utilize infrastructure, hosting providers, APIs, or other services located outside the Philippines.

Consequently, personal information may be processed or stored in other jurisdictions, depending on the service configuration and third-party provider arrangements.

Where international transfers occur, Heroxalis shall seek to ensure that appropriate safeguards and applicable data protection requirements are considered.

Users should consult the relevant project-specific Privacy Policy for additional information concerning international processing arrangements.

9. Data Security

Heroxalis recognizes its responsibility to implement reasonable and appropriate organizational, physical, and technical safeguards for personal information under its control.

Such safeguards may include:

  • Access restrictions and authorization controls.
  • Authentication and account security measures.
  • Appropriate storage and transmission protections.
  • Security monitoring and diagnostic logging.
  • Protection against unauthorized access or disclosure.
  • Reasonable data retention and disposal procedures.
  • Vulnerability identification and remediation.
  • Incident response and investigation procedures.
  • Appropriate safeguards for third-party processing arrangements.

Security measures shall be proportionate to the nature of the information, associated risks, operational requirements, and technical capabilities of the relevant project.

No online service, communication platform, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Users should avoid submitting passwords, authentication tokens, financial credentials, or unnecessary sensitive personal information through Heroxalis services.

10. Personal Data Breach Management

Heroxalis recognizes its responsibilities concerning personal data breaches under applicable data protection laws and regulations.

Where a personal data breach occurs, reasonable steps shall be taken to:

  • Identify and investigate the incident.
  • Assess the nature and extent of the affected information.
  • Contain and mitigate the incident.
  • Implement appropriate corrective measures.
  • Preserve relevant records and documentation.
  • Determine whether notification obligations apply.

Where notification is legally required, the responsible Personal Information Controller shall notify the National Privacy Commission and affected data subjects within the applicable statutory period.

Under the applicable Implementing Rules and Regulations, reportable personal data breaches are generally subject to the seventy-two (72)-hour notification requirement, subject to the legal conditions and exceptions established by the rules.

11. Rights of Data Subjects

In accordance with Section 16 and other applicable provisions of Republic Act No. 10173, individuals whose personal information is processed may exercise the following rights, subject to legal conditions and limitations:

11.1. Right to Be Informed

The right to receive information concerning the collection and processing of personal information.

11.2. Right to Access

The right to request reasonable access to personal information relating to the individual.

11.3. Right to Object

The right to object to certain processing activities where permitted by law.

11.4. Right to Rectification

The right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.

11.5. Right to Erasure or Blocking

The right to request the suspension, withdrawal, blocking, removal, or destruction of personal information under circumstances recognized by law.

11.6. Right to Damages

The right to seek indemnification for damages sustained as a result of inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, false, unlawfully obtained, or unauthorized use of personal information, as provided by law.

11.7. Right to File a Complaint

The right to lodge a complaint before the National Privacy Commission when applicable data privacy rights have been violated.

11.8. Right to Data Portability

The right to obtain personal information in an applicable structured and commonly used electronic format, subject to statutory requirements.

These rights are subject to applicable exceptions, limitations, and procedures established by law.

12. Exercising Privacy Rights and Making Requests

Individuals may submit privacy-related inquiries or requests through the official Heroxalis support channel.

Requests may include:

  • Inquiries concerning the processing of personal information.
  • Requests for access to eligible stored information.
  • Requests for correction of inaccurate information.
  • Requests for deletion, erasure, or blocking where legally applicable.
  • Objections to applicable processing activities.
  • Other requests relating to data subject rights.

Heroxalis may request reasonable information necessary to verify the identity and authority of the requesting individual before processing a request.

Requests shall be evaluated according to applicable law.

Certain information may need to be retained where required for legal compliance, security, operational requirements, or the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims.

Heroxalis projects may integrate with or rely on external platforms and services.

These may include communication platforms, hosting providers, authentication services, AI providers, software libraries, and other technology providers.

Heroxalis does not control all aspects of third-party processing activities and cannot guarantee the practices, availability, or security of independently operated external services.
